# ArcLearn AI – Case Study

## Problem Statement

Students need a smarter and more personalized way to learn. ArcLearn AI helps users through AI tutoring, personalized learning paths, progress tracking, and career recommendations.

## Research Findings

Research was conducted on Coursera, Udemy, and Khan Academy.

### Key Findings

* Simple navigation improves usability.
* Progress tracking increases engagement.
* Personalized learning improves outcomes.
* Mobile accessibility is important.

## User Personas

### College Student

Needs structured learning and practice tests.

### Working Professional

Needs flexible learning and skill development.

### Career Switcher

Needs career guidance and learning roadmaps.

## User Flows

* Sign Up Flow
* Course Enrollment Flow
* AI Tutor Flow
* Progress Tracking Flow

## Design Decisions

* AI Tutor for instant support.
* Dashboard for quick access to learning.
* Analytics for progress tracking.
* Mobile-responsive design for accessibility.

## Final Solution

ArcLearn AI includes:

* AI Tutor Chat
* Learning Paths
* Course Library
* Progress Analytics
* Career Recommendations
* Mobile Experience

## Learnings

* UX Research
* User Flows
* Wireframing
* Design Systems
* Responsive Design
* Prototyping
