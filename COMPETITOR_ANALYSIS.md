# ArcLearn AI – Competitor Analysis

## Platforms Analyzed

* Coursera
* Udemy
* Khan Academy

## Coursera

### Strengths

* Professional courses
* Structured learning

### Weaknesses

* Many paid courses

## Udemy

### Strengths

* Large course library
* Affordable pricing

### Weaknesses

* Course quality varies

## Khan Academy

### Strengths

* Free educational content
* Easy to use

### Weaknesses

* Limited career-focused courses

## Key Insights

* Simple navigation is important.
* Progress tracking motivates learners.
* Personalized recommendations improve engagement.
* Mobile-friendly design enhances accessibility.

## Conclusion

ArcLearn AI combines AI tutoring, personalized learning paths, analytics, and career recommendations to create a modern learning experience.
